Leafspire is a youth-led social impact initiative rooted in Kenya. It works where environmental sustainability, youth leadership, community participation and wellbeing meet, helping young people turn awareness into practical action.

Who Leafspire is

Leafspire works with young people and communities to encourage environmental responsibility, strengthen youth leadership, promote meaningful community participation and support healthier, more connected communities.

We recognise that environmental sustainability, human wellbeing and community development are connected, so our work treats them together.

We create opportunities for young people to move from awareness to action, to help shape their communities and to contribute to a more sustainable future.

The thinking behind the name

Leaf

Nature, growth and renewal

Life, and our responsibility to care for the environment that sustains it.

Spire

Rising and aspiration

Development, and reaching towards greater possibilities for people and places.

Together the two ideas express our belief that nurturing people and caring for the environment are both essential to building a better future. This is the meaning we give the name, not a formal etymology.

Our Mission

To empower young people and communities to create healthier, greener and more resilient futures through environmental action, youth leadership, community participation and integrated wellbeing initiatives.

Our Vision

Communities where young people lead positive change, people thrive, and the environment is valued, protected and sustained for future generations.

Our values

Five commitments guide how we work with young people, communities and the environment.

  1. Environmental Stewardship

    We recognise our responsibility to care for the natural environment and to promote practices that protect ecological wellbeing for present and future generations.

  2. Youth Agency

    We believe young people have the capacity to lead, contribute ideas, take initiative and take part meaningfully in shaping their communities.

  3. Wellbeing and Belonging

    We value healthier relationships, inclusion and social connection, and environments where people feel supported and able to participate.

  4. Accountability

    Meaningful community work calls for responsibility, transparency, learning and respect for the people and communities involved.

  5. Community Partnership

    We value collaboration, local knowledge and the contributions of schools, community groups, institutions and individuals working towards shared goals.

Three areas that depend on one another

Leafspire organises its work around three areas. Each one strengthens the others: a healthier environment supports healthier people, and people who feel they belong are more ready to act together.

Environmental Sustainability and Climate Action

Promoting environmental awareness, responsible resource use, biodiversity, climate action and practical sustainability initiatives.

Youth Leadership and Community Participation

Creating opportunities for young people to develop leadership, contribute ideas, take part in decision-making and act on issues affecting their communities.

Community Wellbeing and Social Connection

Encouraging wellbeing awareness, inclusion, supportive relationships and meaningful social connections.

Our wellbeing work is about awareness and connection. It is not clinical treatment or professional counselling. For more detail, visit our Causes page.

How we approach our work

Five dimensions shape the way Leafspire works, and each one informs the others.

  1. Youth leadership and agency

    Young people take the lead, and their ideas shape what we do.

  2. Environmental stewardship and climate action

    Care for the environment is built into our activities.

  3. Community participation and collaboration

    We work with schools, groups and individuals rather than for them.

  4. Wellbeing, inclusion and social connection

    We want people to feel supported and able to take part.

  5. Learning, innovation and SDG action

    We keep learning, try new ideas and connect local action to the Sustainable Development Goals.
