The people behind Leafspire

One team, working together for young people and the communities they live in. Leafspire is still growing, and the people here are part of an ongoing journey, not a closed list. There is room for others to bring their skills, experience and ideas, and as we develop, new people may join us along the way. Select anyone to read more about them.

  • Escriva Josemaria is a public health professional and social impact leader passionate about empowering young people to become catalysts for change. Through Leafspire, he is building a platform where mentorship, mental wellbeing, leadership and community action come together to help young people discover purpose, develop their potential and create meaningful impact across their communities.

  • Veronicah Leleruk Naitamany is an environmental planner and sustainability advocate committed to creating practical solutions that balance environmental stewardship with community development. She brings a systems-thinking approach to leadership, believing that lasting impact is built through collaboration, innovation and empowering communities to shape a more sustainable future.

  • Faith Nganga is a licensed clinical psychologist dedicated to advancing accessible, compassionate and evidence-based mental health care. With extensive experience supporting children, adolescents and adults, she is passionate about creating safe spaces where individuals can heal, grow and thrive while strengthening the wellbeing of communities.

  • Achsah Otweyo is a quality assurance professional whose work sits at the intersection of environmental sustainability, public health and scientific excellence. Passionate about building efficient systems and promoting safe, sustainable communities, she brings a detail-oriented approach to organisational operations while championing solutions that create lasting social and environmental impact.

  • Jane Musembi is a data analyst and development professional committed to using evidence and research to strengthen community programmes and inform better decision-making. She is passionate about leveraging data to create sustainable solutions that improve lives and drive social impact.

  • Maryanne Onyango is a safety and operations professional with a background in project management, operational planning and risk management. She is driven by a commitment to building resilient communities and supporting initiatives that create lasting positive change.

  • Mercy Stella Wangui is a development practitioner dedicated to advancing community-led solutions through collaboration, innovation and inclusive partnerships. She believes that sustainable impact is achieved by empowering communities and fostering collective action toward shared goals.

  • Teddy Ayodi is an emerging environmental innovator and aspiring entrepreneur with a passion for sustainable development and technology-driven solutions. Teams form around the work

Leafspire has a small leadership team and a growing network of volunteers, interns and young leaders. Instead of a fixed hierarchy, each project brings together the people it needs at the time.

Shared responsibility

Founders, project leads and volunteers each carry part of the work, and teams change as a project’s needs change.

Youth leadership

Young leaders guide each initiative, because the people closest to a challenge often hold the most useful answers.

Different skills together

Public health, planning, psychology, data, communications and operations all sit within this team, and projects draw on the mix they need.

Community and environment

Work starts with what a community needs, and with what its local environment can support.

“We started Leafspire because we refused to accept that young people lack potential. They lack platforms, and that’s exactly what we are building.”

Escriva JosemariaFounder & Executive Director
