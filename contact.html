What you can write to us about

Leafspire works across environmental action, youth leadership and community wellbeing in Kenya. If your question touches any of that, we would like to hear it.

  • Volunteering and youth participationWays young people can take part in activities and community action.
  • Schools and community groupsWorking with us on environmental learning, tree growing and local projects.
  • Partnerships and programme ideasCollaboration with organisations, institutions and environmental partners.
  • Donations and merchandiseQuestions about supporting Leafspire or ordering merchandise.
  • Media, stories and everything elseIf it does not fit above, write to us anyway.
