Wear your support.

Leafspire merchandise is a way to carry the initiative with you, show your connection to our youth and community work, and help us keep it going.

See the Collection Make an Enquiry

Merchandise is one of the ways you can stand with Leafspire.

Leafspire is a youth-led initiative rooted in Kenya, working on environmental action, youth leadership and community wellbeing. We are not a clothing business.

Read the stories and programmes behind the name.

Supporting us does not have to mean a donation

Carry the identity.

Each piece features the Leafspire name or emblem, so you can wear or carry something that stands for what we do.

Back the work.

Your support helps us continue developing our youth and community development work.

Show you belong.

Merchandise is another way to show your connection to the initiative, alongside getting involved in other ways.

Merchandise is not a tax-deductible donation. If you would rather give directly, you can support Leafspire here.
