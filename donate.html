Support Leafspire

Give what helps people and communities grow

Leafspire's work spans youth development, education, environmental action and community wellbeing, and many kinds of in-kind and material contributions can support it. You might have furniture, equipment, books, learning materials, stationery, water, useful household items or something else entirely. If you would like to give something, we would love to hear about it, even if it is not mentioned here.

We are not currently accepting monetary donations, including cash, bank transfers or mobile money, because we have not yet put the right structures in place to receive and manage them. That reflects our current arrangements, and for now we welcome non-monetary contributions.

Why in-kind, for now

Support should reach the work, with nothing lost on the way

Practical contributions can be put straight to use: a seedling goes in the ground, a bucket goes to a clean-up. They also keep things simple and transparent, because Leafspire is not asking anyone to send money to an individual or to a channel that has not been properly set up.

That is a deliberate choice about accountability, and we would rather get it right than rush it.

How to offer a contribution

The easiest way to start is to send us an email. Tell us what you would like to contribute and we will take it from there.

  1. Send us an email

    Write to leafspirefoundation@gmail.com and tell us a little about what you have in mind. It does not need to be detailed or formal.

  2. We talk it through

    We will reply to discuss your idea and how it could be useful to our work.

  3. We sort out the practicalities together

    If it works for both sides, we will agree how and when the contribution can reach us. Please check with us before sending anything.

Questions you may have

More answers are on our FAQs page.

Is Leafspire accepting money?

Not at this stage. We are welcoming in-kind contributions while we establish an appropriate and accountable institutional arrangement for financial donations. Please do not send money to any person or account in Leafspire's name.

Will Leafspire accept everything that is offered?

Not always. Relevance, condition, safety, suitability and actual programme needs are considered before a contribution is accepted. We would rather say no politely than accept something we cannot use well.

Can I offer something that is not mentioned on this page?

Yes. The examples on this page only show the range of possibilities. Get in touch and tell us what you would like to contribute, and we can talk it through.

What if I would like to support Leafspire financially?

Please contact us for an update on our position. Do not send money to any individual or account.

Can a school, business or group contribute?

Yes. Households, schools, businesses and community groups are all welcome to offer resources they already have or can source.

Have something that could help? Let us know.

Tell us what you have and we will tell you honestly whether it fits our work right now.
