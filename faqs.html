Understanding Leafspire

Who we are, what our name and tagline mean, where we are based and our current legal status.

Leafspire is a youth-led social impact initiative rooted in Kenya. We work with young people, schools and communities to turn what people learn into practical action close to home.

Our work connects three areas: environmental sustainability and climate action, youth leadership and community participation, and community wellbeing and social connection. They are meant to support one another, not to run as separate projects.

Read more about Leafspire

The name carries intended symbolism rather than a formal definition or a historical origin. A leaf stands for growth, nature and renewal. A spire points upwards, suggesting aspiration and reaching higher.

Put together, the two words describe what we hope to see: people and communities growing, with young people leading the way and caring for the environment they share.

“Growing People” is about the development of young people: their confidence, leadership skills, sense of belonging and sense of responsibility for the place they live in.

“Greening Communities” is about environmental care, such as tree growing and looking after shared spaces, and about communities becoming healthier and better connected.

The two halves depend on each other. Young people who feel supported are more likely to take care of their surroundings, and a shared, cared-for environment gives people a reason to come together and work as a team.

Leafspire began as an idea about young people coming together and connecting with one another. As that idea developed, it grew into a wider youth-led social impact initiative.

Its purpose now includes youth leadership, environmental sustainability, community participation and a shared responsibility for the future. Leafspire was started by four young Kenyans, and you can meet them on our Our Team page.

Leafspire is rooted in Kenya. Our initial programme development and pilot direction is in Trans Nzoia County, subject to partnerships and resources.

We do not publish an office address, and we are not presenting Leafspire as having established programmes in several locations. The best way to reach us is by email or phone, and the details are in the contact section below.

No. Leafspire is currently an unregistered initiative. It is not at present a registered NGO, PBO, CBO, charity, foundation or company, and contributions to it should not be treated as tax-deductible.

What we are is a youth-led initiative with a clear mission, building its work step by step. Our longer-term ambition is to grow into an international NGO, but that is a goal for the future and not our legal status today.

Our Causes and Approach

The three areas we work in, why they belong together and which global goals they relate to.

Our work is organised around three interconnected areas:

  • Environmental Sustainability & Climate Action: environmental education, tree growing, school greening and stewardship of local environments.
  • Youth Leadership & Community Participation: supporting young people to identify community needs, take responsibility and help shape solutions.
  • Community Wellbeing & Social Connection: creating supportive spaces where people connect, feel included and learn about wellbeing.

Explore our causes

Healthy communities depend on both. A clean, green and well-cared-for environment supports people’s health and daily life, while good relationships and a sense of belonging are what make people willing to look after shared spaces over the long term.

We also believe that young people and communities should help shape a healthier, more sustainable future rather than simply receive it. Working on the environment and on social connection together gives people more reasons to take part, and more reasons to keep going.

Our work relates most closely to these goals:

  • SDG 3, Good Health and Well-being, through our wellbeing and social connection work.
  • SDG 4, Quality Education, through environmental learning and youth leadership.
  • SDG 11, Sustainable Cities and Communities, through community participation and resilience.
  • SDG 13, Climate Action, through environmental education and local action.
  • SDG 15, Life on Land, through tree growing and biodiversity awareness.
  • SDG 17, Partnerships for the Goals, through collaboration with schools, communities and organisations.

These are goals our work is oriented towards. Leafspire has no formal affiliation with, accreditation from or partnership with the United Nations.

Leafspire is youth-led, which means young people are at the centre of how our work is shaped and carried out. It does not mean they work alone.

Lasting community change involves schools, families, educators, community members and other stakeholders, and our approach is to work alongside them. Young people lead; the wider community takes part.

Our Programmes

The five programme directions we are developing, how far along they are and how they differ.

We are developing five programme directions:

  • Leafspire Green Schools Initiative: school-based environmental education, school greening and student environmental leadership.
  • Youth in Action Programme: practical, youth-led initiatives that young people plan and carry out in their communities.
  • Community Wellbeing Programme: wellbeing awareness, social connection, supportive activities and inclusion.
  • Community Sustainability & Resilience Programme: local sustainability practices that help communities adapt to environmental and social pressures.
  • Youth Engagement & Participation Programme: opportunities for young people to contribute their voices and views to community decisions.

Safeguarding, Privacy and Accountability

How we think about child safety, personal information and raising a concern.

Much of our work involves young people and schools, so protecting children and young people matters to us. That means treating safeguarding seriously, respecting personal boundaries and acting to prevent harm.

We have not published a dedicated reporting line or named safeguarding officer on this website, and we do not claim any safeguarding certification. If you have a concern about a child’s safety, please contact us by email or phone. If a child is at immediate risk, contact local emergency services or the relevant child protection authorities straight away.

Our Legal & Governance page is where governance information is published.

Personal information, such as an email address given when you subscribe to updates or contact us, should be handled responsibly and in line with the privacy and data protection requirements that apply.

For more information, see our Legal & Governance page. We do not claim a Data Protection Officer, a data protection registration or specific security certifications.

Please contact us directly by email at leafspirefoundation@gmail.com or by phone on +254 701 114562. Tell us what happened, or what you would like us to know, and include contact details if you would like a reply.

We do not operate a formal complaints platform or case-management system, and we cannot promise a fixed response time. We do take feedback seriously, and we would rather hear a concern than not hear it.
