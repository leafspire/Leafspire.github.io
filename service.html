OurProgrammes

Leafspire works through six connected programme areas. Together they support young people, strengthen the communities they live in and advance sustainable development, starting with practical action close to home.

The six programmes

  1. 01

    Green Schooled

    Environmental learning, school greening and tree growing in schools and higher education institutions, led by students.Includes Eco-Schools and Eco-Campus
  2. 02

    The Next Meta

    Supports young people to develop ideas, build entrepreneurial skills and explore sustainable livelihoods.
  3. 03

    SusComm

    Community greening, biodiversity, waste management, climate resilience and food security through practical local action.
  4. 04

    Youth 4 Youth

    Conversations, peer connection and awareness on youth mental health and wellbeing, with routes to appropriate support.
  5. 05

    Afya Mtaani with Leafspire

    Community health awareness and preventive education, delivered with qualified health professionals and partners.
  6. 06

    Civic-lore

    Civic education that supports public participation, youth voice in decisions and constructive engagement with institutions.

Our Campaigns

Alongside our programmes, these campaigns bring people together around food, stories, nature, creativity and animals.

  • 01

    Share a Plate

    Community cooking, food-sharing gatherings and activities promoting food appreciation, nutrition awareness and community connection.

  • 02

    Changemakers Spotlight

    A storytelling and recognition campaign highlighting young people and other community members making positive contributions in their communities.

  • 03

    We Outside

    A campaign bringing young people together through outdoor experiences, exploration, nature activities, educational outings and community experiences.

  • 04

    Make It Matter

    A practical creativity and sustainability campaign encouraging repair, reuse, upcycling and turning discarded or underused materials into useful products and ideas.

  • 05

    Your Friendly Neighbourhood

    An animal welfare campaign promoting compassion, responsible care, protection and awareness across different animal species, from donkeys, cats and stray birds to livestock and wildlife.
